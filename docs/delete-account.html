Delete your OverlAI account

OverlAI (by Lerbernard) lets you permanently delete your account and the data associated with it at any time, directly inside the app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open OverlAI and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the ACCOUNT section.
  3. Make sure you are signed in, then tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Can't access the app?

Email OverlAI.support@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account", and we will delete it for you.

What gets deleted

  • Your authentication record (Google account link) - deleted immediately.
  • Your email address and user ID stored with your account - deleted immediately.
  • Your premium status record - deleted immediately.

What is kept

Nothing is retained on our servers after deletion - there is no additional retention period.

Detection history and settings are stored only on your device and are not part of your account. They remain on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall.

For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
