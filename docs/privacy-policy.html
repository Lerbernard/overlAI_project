OverlAI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

OverlAI ("the app", "we") helps you check whether images and videos are AI-generated. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Images and videos you check. When you run a detection, that media is uploaded to Sightengine, a third-party detection service, and analyzed there. Sightengine's own privacy policy governs that processing. We do not store your media on any server we control.
  • Account information (optional). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and a Google account identifier and store them via Firebase Authentication and Firestore (both provided by Google) to recognize you and remember your premium status across devices. Sign-in is optional; detection works without an account.
  • Advertising data. For non-premium users, the app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, per Google's advertising policies.
  • On-device data. Detection results, History thumbnails, monthly usage counts, and settings are stored only on your device.

How screen capture works

Screen capture happens only when you trigger it, and Android requests your permission each time. The overlay permission is used solely to draw the floating bubble over other apps.

Data sharing

We share the media you check with Sightengine for the sole purpose of analysis, and use Google (Firebase, AdMob) as service providers as described above. We do not sell your data.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete History entries, clear the app's data, or uninstall. If you created an account, you can permanently delete it and its stored premium flag from the app's Settings; this removes your record from our Firebase database and deletes your authentication entry. You may also email us to request deletion.

Children

OverlAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
